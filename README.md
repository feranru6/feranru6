## Hi there 👋

The documents included in this repository consist of documents whose contents are described below:
A macroeconomic model of the Bolivian economy, developed using System Dynamics tools, which integrates the different components of the economy from the supply side, including production, capital stock, and labor, as well as from the demand side, including consumption, investment, public expenditure, and international trade. The model also incorporates the national and international financial blocks, the trade balance and exchange rate, the money supply, interest rates, inflation, and related variables, thereby enabling the simulation of different policy scenarios.

A model of the functioning of the chicken market in Bolivia, developed using System Dynamics tools, which reproduces price oscillations by integrating supply dynamics related to chicken rearing, the growth and development of birds until they become broiler chicken stocks for the market, population growth and consumption, the relationship between inventories and their influence on prices, and the interaction of these variables with supply and demand. The model also includes material delays associated with the growth and development of the bird population, as well as information delays in stakeholders’ decision-making processes based on price evolution.

A compartmentalization and classification model for 53 industrial projects, using the k-means algorithm based on nine characteristics: investment amount, degree of progress, debts owed to financial institutions, debts owed to third parties, status of land ownership rights (given that these are state projects), logistical status, access to raw materials, access to markets, and strategic position. The purpose of this model is to apply policies and actions to similar groups in accordance with the established stratification.

An optimization problem using mathematical models in the field of nonlinear programming to determine the optimal combination of products for a textile company. The model considers the maximization of the difference between sales and costs, financing through different credit systems, product sales, and a business risk threshold, using Excel Solver.

A sectoral diagnosis of the manufacturing industry in Bolivia and a multiplier analysis based on input-output matrix data, as well as investment modeling criteria. This document presents an analysis of sectoral information, the classification of the industry, and its interlinkages.
